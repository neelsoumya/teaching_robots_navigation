# Safety and Ethics

## Safety

- HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study) methodology

- `Deviation`: barrel not gripped

- `Possible causes`: attach failed

- `Consequences`: an item left unattanded

- `Mitigating actions`: tried grabbing again


- real world deployment will require obstacle avoidance especially with humans around

- hardware stop/override

- multiple sensors to detect humans

- SIL2/SIL3 safety integrity levels



## Ethics

- a robot operating in a haazrdous environment can spread contamination or make it more unsafe

- stopping and human oversight

- can displace human workers

- need to retrain them

- transparency and explainability

- should not _overtrust_ system
