From waypoints to wheel velocities

A path planner only says where to go: a list of (x, y) points. The controller has to turn that into two numbers the motors understand, a left wheel speed and a right wheel speed. Click the arena to add points and follow the numbers.

Click to add waypoints. Red dashed line: direction to the target. Dark line: where the robot points. The arc is the heading error e.

How the conversion works

        
    
          
  1. Compare the target with the robot: distance d and heading error e.
    2. 
          
  2. Pick a forward speed v (faster when far) and a turn rate ω (faster when e is large).
    3. 
          
  3. Split v and ω into two wheel speeds.
    4. 
        

        
v = (vL + vR) / 2
ω = (vR − vL) / L
so:
vL = v − ω·L/2
vR = v + ω·L/2

        
L is the distance between the wheels. The average of the wheels moves the robot forward. Their difference spins it.
